# User Guide

## Getting started
1. Open the site and click **Join free**. Fill in your name, username, email, branch, year and a strong password. Choose *Senior / Alumni* if you want to mentor juniors.
2. Enter the **6-digit code** emailed to you (in development it is printed in the server terminal). Codes expire after 10 minutes; use **Resend code** if needed.
3. You land on your **Dashboard**: counters, quick actions, potential lost-and-found matches, new marketplace items, announcements and notifications.
4. Update your photo, bio, phone and LinkedIn from the avatar menu → **My profile → Edit profile**.

## Lost & Found
- **Report:** *I lost something* / *I found something* → item name, category, where, when, description, optional photo.
- **Search:** type in the search bar or change filters — results update instantly.
- **Matches:** when your item resembles an opposite report, you get a notification and the match appears on your item page and dashboard with a % score.
- **Contact:** open an item and click the contact button to start a private chat. Ask for identifying details before handing items over.
- **Manage:** on your item page → *Mark as recovered*, *Edit* or delete. *My lost & found* lists all your reports.

## Marketplace
- **Sell:** *Sell an item* → title, category, condition, price (₹), description, up to 3 photos.
- **Browse:** search, filter by category/condition/price, sort, optionally include sold items.
- **Save:** click the ♥ on any card; see them in *Saved*.
- **Enquire:** on a product page write a message (and optional offer) → the seller is notified and a chat opens.
- **Seller tools:** set *Available / Reserved / Sold*, edit, delete; *My listings* shows saves and enquiries.
- **Report:** use *Report this listing* for scams or prohibited items. There are no online payments — meet in public campus spaces.

## Find a Roommate
1. **Create my profile** with budget, preferred area, accommodation type, food, sleep schedule and cleanliness.
2. Browse profiles sorted by **best match**; each card shows a compatibility ring and which preferences match.
3. Open a profile for the full breakdown (budget 30, location 25, food 15, sleep 15, cleanliness 15).
4. **Send roommate request** with a short intro. The receiver can accept or reject under *Requests*; accepted pairs can message each other.
5. Untick *Show my profile to others* when you've found someone.

## Campus Locator
- Search by room number (`A310`), lab name (`AI`), faculty name, department, or use the quick chips (Library, Cafeteria, Labs, Faculty).
- Click a result to fly the map to the building and see floor, department and faculty details.
- The demo campus is fictional; administrators replace it with real data.

## Connect with Seniors
- Search by name/company, filter by **skill**, **domain** and **batch**.
- Open a profile to read their introduction and achievements, open LinkedIn/GitHub, and **Request mentorship** with a topic and message.
- Track status under *My requests*.
- **Seniors:** create your profile via *My senior profile*; review requests in *Mentee requests* and accept/reject with an optional note. Toggle *Accepting new requests* off when busy.

## Announcements & Events
Browse pinned updates, filter by category or *Upcoming events*, and use **Register** links for events.

## Notifications & Messages
- The bell shows unread alerts (refreshes automatically). Click one to open the related page; *Mark all read* clears the badge. The **Notification center** lists everything.
- The chat icon opens your **Messages**. Conversations are private to the two participants; press Enter to send.

## Administrators
Avatar menu → **Admin dashboard**:
- **Overview:** KPIs and charts, date range filter, **Export CSV** (summary, students, products, lost & found, roommates, mentorship, reports).
- **Moderation:** hide, dismiss or delete reported listings; restore hidden products; remove lost-and-found posts.
- **Students:** search, change role, verify email, suspend/reactivate.
- **Buildings & rooms:** add/edit/delete buildings (with coordinates), rooms and departments.
- **Announcements:** create, edit, pin, delete, and optionally notify all students.
- **Django admin** (`/django-admin/`) offers full raw data management.

## Safety tips
Never share passwords or OTPs. Meet in public places for exchanges. Report suspicious listings. Only share information you're comfortable making visible to other students.
